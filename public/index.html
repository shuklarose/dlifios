What are the key provisions of the AI Act?

The EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) introduces a risk-based framework for AI systems. Key provisions include: • Prohibition of unacceptable-risk AI practices (e.g., social scoring by public authorities). • High-risk AI systems must meet strict requirements for transparency, human oversight, and robustness. • Obligations for transparency in AI systems interacting with individuals. • Establishment of governance at EU and national levels. The Act is phased in from August 2024 and fully applicable by August 2026.
