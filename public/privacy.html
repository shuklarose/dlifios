Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

DlíFios answers questions about EU law. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to get it deleted. It describes what the software actually does — each item below maps to a real field in our database or a real outbound request in our code.

1. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address Sign-up It is your login. We send a one-time link to it; there is no password.
Full name Sign-up To address you in the app and in emails.
Organisation, company size, sector, phone (all optional) Sign-up To tailor the weekly digest to your industry. Leave them blank and the service works identically.
Your questions Each time you ask To count usage against your daily limit, and to measure and improve answer quality.
IP address Only when signed out To limit anonymous use to 3 questions a day. Held in server memory only, never written to the database, and lost whenever the server restarts.

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party pixels. The only thing stored in your browser is your own login session.

2. Who else sees it

ProcessorWhat they receive
Supabase — authentication and database Your email, name and the optional profile fields; your questions.
Google (Gemini) — generates the answers The text of your question, plus the legal passages we retrieved. Your name, email and account identity are not sent.
Qdrant — vector search Nothing personal. It stores only published EU legal texts.

Worth knowing: your question text is sent to Google to produce the answer. Please don't put personal data about yourself or anyone else into a question — describe the situation in general terms instead. That is good practice with any AI assistant, not just this one.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the processors above.

3. Legal basis

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — your account, your questions, and enforcing usage limits. Without these the service cannot function.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — the weekly digest email. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — the anonymous IP rate limit, to protect the service from abuse and runaway cost.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile — until you delete your account.
  • Questions — until you delete your account.
  • IP addresses — in memory only, for at most 24 hours, and cleared on restart.

Deleting your account removes all of the above immediately. There is no soft-delete or hidden archive.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your national data-protection authority.

Delete your account right now: sign in, then use Delete account next to your name in the top-right. It erases your profile, your questions and your login in one step, with no waiting period and no email exchange.

Prefer to ask a human, or want a copy of your data? Email ritik135001@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, as Article 12(3) requires.

6. Security

  • No passwords exist. Sign-in is by one-time email link, so there is no password to leak.
  • Row Level Security is enforced on every database table — your profile is readable only by you.
  • The usage log is locked so that no browser can read it; only our server can write to it.
  • All connections use TLS.

No system is perfectly secure. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to the address above rather than disclosing it publicly, and we will fix it.

7. Not legal advice

DlíFios states what the law says and cites its sources. It does not advise you on what to do in your situation, and using it does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Answers are generated by an AI model from official EU texts and can be incomplete or wrong — always verify against the linked source before relying on anything.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Material changes affecting how we use your data will be emailed to registered users.
